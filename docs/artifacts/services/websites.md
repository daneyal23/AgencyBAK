# Websites

## Tagline

A site that earns the next conversation.

## The stop line

A website that looks finished but does not create a booked call, a WhatsApp reply, or a CRM lead is decoration. We stop when the site is fast, clear on one primary ask, and wired to the system where your team actually follows up — and when you own the codebase or CMS.

## In plain English

Your site’s job is not to impress peers. It is to move a serious buyer from “who are you?” to “talk to us” — with enough proof that the ask feels earned. We design and build for one primary CTA, short paths to contact, and tracking that lands in your CRM or WhatsApp — not a black-box agency form that disappears into someone else’s inbox.

You own the codebase or CMS. We do not hold the keys. Updates, hosting decisions, and future vendors stay under your control across the US, Canada, UK, Ireland, EU, Australia, New Zealand, and the GCC.

## Who this is for

- Food B2B sellers (catering, wholesale, corporate, events) who need trust and a clear next step — not a food-blog template
- Traditional SMEs and B2B service firms whose site still says “welcome” instead of “book a call / message us”
- Teams aligning web with outbound: same offer, same proof, same accounts
- Owners who want published clarity and client-owned assets — not an opaque agency retainer forever

## What you get

- Information architecture and copy focused on one primary conversion
- A fast front end with mobile-first layout and accessible basics
- Primary CTA wired to CRM, calendar, or WhatsApp (as fits your market)
- Secondary paths that do not compete with the main ask
- Analytics events that map to real outcomes (form submit, book, WhatsApp click) — not vanity scroll depth alone
- Handoff: repo or CMS access, deploy notes, and how to change pages without us
- Optional alignment with your GTM list and sequences so the site speaks to the same buyers you email

## Benefits

- Visitors who fit your ICP know what to do in one glance
- Leads land where sales already works — CRM or WhatsApp — not a forgotten mailbox
- Speed and clarity reduce bounce from serious buyers on mobile
- You can change offers and proof without waiting on an agency gatekeeper
- Web stops being a separate brand project and becomes part of the same GTM motion

## Under the hood (technical)

- Stack chosen for ownership: modern static/SSR or CMS you control; no proprietary lock-in theme you cannot export
- Performance budget: lean assets, sensible image handling, Core Web Vitals treated as product requirements
- Conversion wiring: form → CRM fields; calendar embed; WhatsApp deep links with UTM or equivalent attribution where useful
- Event schema aligned to pipeline language (inquiry, booked, held) — not only “session”
- SEO foundations: titles, headings, crawlable structure; no fake “rank #1” promises
- Access: client admin, hosting, DNS, and repo (when code-based) in your accounts from day one

## How it works (finish → start)

1. **Finish** — Live site on your domain; one primary CTA working end-to-end into CRM/WhatsApp/calendar; you have admin access; deploy or publish path documented.
2. **QA** — Speed, mobile, forms, and handoff training so your team can edit without breaking the funnel.
3. **Connect** — Build integrations; instrument the events that matter for sales follow-up.
4. **Build** — Structure and write for the offer and proof that match your GTM — not generic industry filler.
5. **Start** — Clarify audience, primary CTA, and where conversations already happen (CRM, phone, WhatsApp). Kill competing asks early.

## What you own

- Domain, hosting, CMS or repository, and design assets produced for the site
- Form endpoints, CRM objects/fields, and WhatsApp business assets used in the funnel
- Analytics properties and conversion events
- The right to migrate, redesign, or hire someone else without reclaiming “the agency site”

## What we do not do

- Launch a brochure with five equal CTAs and no follow-up path
- Hold the site hostage on an agency-only platform or unpaid “maintenance” lock
- Prioritize awards aesthetics over load time and the next conversation
- Sell vanity metrics (traffic spikes, bounce rate theater) as success without pipeline wiring
- Build in a vacuum from outbound and CRM so the site sells a different company than your emails

## Three lenses (close)

- **Technical writer:** The deliverable is a fast, owned site with one primary CTA and documented wiring into CRM or WhatsApp — not a concept mock that never ships.
- **Advertisement:** A site that earns the next conversation — clear ask, real proof, same story your outbound already tells.
- **Winner:** Own the codebase or CMS; measure the click that becomes a held conversation; ignore design that does not sell.
