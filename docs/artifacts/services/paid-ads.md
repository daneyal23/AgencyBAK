# Paid ads

## Tagline

Spend that talks to the same accounts as your outbound.

## The stop line

Ads that chase cold volume while outbound works a different list are two companies arguing inside one budget. We stop when paid, email, and calls share the same TAM — and when the ad accounts, pixels, and audiences sit in your name.

## In plain English

Paid ads here means buying attention from the accounts you already decided to sell to — not renting strangers to inflate clicks. We align audiences, creative, and landing paths with your GTM list and offer so a prospect can see you in the inbox and in the feed without hearing two different pitches.

You own the ad accounts. We operate inside them. When the engagement ends, the history, audiences, and learning stay with you — across markets in the US, Canada, UK, Ireland, EU, Australia, New Zealand, and the GCC (with channel choice fit to each market’s norms, including WhatsApp-aware paths where that is how buyers reply).

## Who this is for

- Food B2B and B2B service firms that already (or soon will) run outbound and want paid to reinforce the same accounts
- Traditional SMEs ready to spend only when the offer and landing path are clear
- Teams who refuse “agency-owned” ad accounts and opaque media markups as a business model
- Operators who care about meetings and pipeline — not vanity CTR screenshots

## What you get

- Audience design from the same TAM and ICP filters as outbound (account lists, lookalikes only when they serve that TAM — not random expansion)
- Creative and copy that match sequence messaging and site CTA
- Channel plan fit to market and buyer (e.g. Meta, LinkedIn, Google — chosen for reach of *your* accounts, not habit)
- Landing path into owned site, CRM, calendar, or WhatsApp
- Measurement tied to inquiry / book / held — not clicks alone
- Account setup or audit in *your* Business Manager / Ads accounts
- A kill-or-scale rule set: what we pause when cost-per-conversation is wrong

## Benefits

- Budget compounds on the same accounts your sales team already recognizes
- Creative tests teach message truth that feeds back into email and calls
- No asset orphaning: audiences and pixel history remain yours
- Less waste on broad “awareness” that never enters your CRM stages
- Paid becomes a controlled layer on GTM — not a separate agency P&L

## Under the hood (technical)

- Account ownership: client as admin; agency as operator with least-privilege access
- Audience plumbing: matched lists / CRM segments where platforms allow; exclusion of customers and anti-ICP
- UTM and event discipline aligned with CRM stages and site events
- Creative variants mapped to offer angles already validated (or under the ~50-email proof logic from strategy — not invented in the ads silo)
- Budget pacing with explicit caps and pause criteria tied to conversation cost, not “always on”
- Compliance and market norms: disclosure, restricted categories, and channel fit (especially where WhatsApp or local platforms matter more than LinkedIn)

## How it works (finish → start)

1. **Finish** — Campaigns live in your accounts; audiences reflect the shared TAM; conversions report into your CRM language; pause/scale rules are written.
2. **Optimize** — On conversation quality and list overlap with outbound — not on vanity engagement.
3. **Launch** — Matched messaging to site and sequences; verify events and lead routing.
4. **Build** — Audiences and creative from the agreed ICP and offer — not from a generic industry template.
5. **Start** — Confirm account ownership, TAM source (list/CRM), primary CTA, and markets. No spend until the destination and follow-up path work.

## What you own

- Ad accounts, Business Manager (or equivalent), pixels, tags, and custom audiences
- Creative files and copy produced for the campaigns
- Conversion events and reporting definitions
- Historical performance data after we leave

## What we do not do

- Run spend from agency-owned accounts you cannot export
- Optimize for vanity metrics (likes, cheap clicks, impressions) as the success story
- Broad “spray” audiences that ignore your outbound TAM
- Hide markups or refuse to show platform invoices in your accounts
- Scale paid hard while the offer is still unproven and the landing/CRM path is broken

## Three lenses (close)

- **Technical writer:** Paid is configured in client-owned accounts with audiences, events, and pause rules documented against the same TAM as outbound.
- **Advertisement:** Spend that talks to the same accounts as your outbound — one message, one list logic, measurable conversations.
- **Winner:** Own the ad accounts; pay only to reach the accounts you intend to close; cut anything that does not earn the next conversation.
