# SEO

## Tagline

Found when they’re already looking — and ready to talk.

## The stop line

Rankings without a path into your CRM are vanity. We build local and service SEO so the enquiry lands where you sell — not in a forgotten inbox.

## In plain English

Someone searches for what you do, near where you do it. They find you. They ask a question. That ask becomes a deal in your CRM — with a source, an owner, and a next step.

That is the whole job. Not “content calendar.” Not a screenshot of page-two keywords. A searcher who was already buying, routed into the system you actually use.

We focus on local SEO (maps, Google Business Profile, city/service pages) and service SEO (the phrases people type when they need what you sell). We wire the form, call, WhatsApp, or booking button so nothing dies on a “thank you” page.

You have a real offer and a real territory. You want demand that was looking for you, not cold outreach dressed as “organic.”

## Under the hood (technical)

We start from Search Console, Analytics, and (for local) GBP insights — not from a keyword tool fantasy list. Technical work covers crawl budget waste, broken canonicals, thin location clones, and mobile UX that kills conversion.

Service and location pages get structured data where Google uses it. Internal linking mirrors how you sell. GBP is treated as a product surface: NAP consistency, service categories, Q&A, photo cadence, review request flow.

Tracking: dedicated conversion events, call tracking when it pays for itself, enquiry → CRM contact/deal with campaign and landing-page attribution. If ranking rose but CRM did not, we treat that as a failure of the system — not a win.

## How it works (finish → start)

1. **Finish** — Enquiries create or update CRM records; you see source, page, and status. You can close the loop without asking “who emailed us?”
2. **Operate** — Monthly: technical fixes, page upgrades, GBP hygiene, content that supports money queries — measured against enquiry volume and quality, not rank vanity alone.
3. **Capture** — Forms, click-to-call, WhatsApp, and booking buttons post into CRM with attribution. Thank-you states do not orphan the lead.
4. **Build** — Money pages and GBP assets ship against the intent map. Thin or duplicate pages get cut or merged.
5. **Start** — Audit: Search Console, site crawl, GBP, competitor SERPs for your real services, and how enquiries flow today (or fail to).

## Step by step

The full delivery, in order. Each step says what we do, what we need from you, and what you have at the end of it.

### 1. Audit

- **We do:** Review Google Search Console, Analytics, a full site crawl, your Google Business Profile, the search results for your real services, and how enquiries flow (or fail to) into your CRM today.
- **You provide:** Access to Search Console, Analytics and your Google Business Profile.
- **You get:** An audit with ranked fixes, starting with what blocks enquiries.

### 2. Keyword and intent map

- **We do:** Map the searches buyers actually make: service × location, and commercial (ready to buy) vs informational (still learning). Each important search gets one page, so your own pages do not compete with each other (keyword cannibalization).
- **You get:** A map of which page should win which search.

### 3. Technical fixes

- **We do:** Fix indexation (pages Google can and cannot see), canonicals (telling Google which version of a page is the main one), XML sitemaps, redirects, speed, mobile usability and internal links. We add schema markup (for example local business and service) where search engines use it.
- **You get:** A site search engines can crawl and understand.

### 4. Money pages

- **We do:** Build or rewrite the service and location pages that earn enquiries, each with unique content, proof and one clear CTA. Thin or duplicate location pages are merged or removed.
- **You get:** Pages built to turn searches into conversations.

### 5. Google Business Profile and local signals

- **We do:** Set the right categories and services, keep NAP consistent (name, address, phone, the same everywhere online), clean up citations (listings on other directories), and add photos, posts and Q&A. We set up a review request flow after each job.
- **You get:** A stronger chance of appearing in the local map results.

### 6. Capture and attribution

- **We do:** Wire forms, click-to-call, WhatsApp and booking buttons into the CRM with the landing page and source attached. Where it pays for itself, we add call tracking with dynamic numbers, so phone leads are credited to the right page.
- **You get:** Every organic enquiry in the CRM with where it came from.

### 7. Supporting content

- **We do:** Write content that answers the questions buyers ask before choosing a supplier, and link it to the money pages. No posts written just to fill a calendar.
- **You get:** Content that supports pages that sell.

### 8. Monthly operate

- **We do:** Each month, run technical fixes, page upgrades, profile upkeep and new content, measured against enquiry volume and quality.
- **You get:** A monthly report from impressions through to CRM deals.

## Technical terms, in plain words

| Term | What it means |
|---|---|
| SERP | The search results page. |
| Search intent | What the searcher is trying to do: learn, compare, or buy now. |
| Search Console | Google's free tool showing how your site appears in search. |
| Crawl | Search engines reading your site's pages. |
| Indexation | Whether a page is stored by Google and can appear in results. |
| Canonical | A tag telling Google which version of similar pages is the main one. |
| Keyword cannibalization | Two of your own pages competing for the same search. |
| Schema markup | Code that tells search engines what a page is about. |
| Google Business Profile | Your listing on Google Maps and in local results. |
| Local pack | The map with three businesses shown at the top of local searches. |
| NAP | Name, address, phone: should be identical everywhere online. |
| Citation | A listing of your business on another website or directory. |
| Internal linking | Links between your own pages that show search engines what matters most. |
| Backlink | A link to your site from another website. |
| Call tracking | Phone numbers that show which page or source a call came from. |
| XML sitemap | A file listing your pages so search engines can find them. |
