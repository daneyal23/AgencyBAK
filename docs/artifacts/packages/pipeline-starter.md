# Pipeline Starter

## Tagline

Who to sell. What to say. Meetings that show up.

## For whom

Owner-led food businesses chasing catering / corporate accounts, and traditional SMEs or B2B service firms that need a runnable ICP and a CRM that tells the truth — without a full GTM engineering embed.

## What’s in

| Capability | Role in the package |
|------------|---------------------|
| GTM strategy | ICP, account tiers, messages, explicit ignore list |
| Outbound & lead gen | Domains/inboxes you own, sequences, booked **and** held reporting |
| CRM & RevOps | Stages and fields for contacted → replied → booked → held → pipeline |

## What’s out

GTM engineering depth (Clay signal systems, multi-channel orchestration), paid ads, SEO programs, full website rebuilds, heavy AI agent fleets. Those live in GTM Machine or Demand System.

## Commercial (USD starting — draft)

| Layer | Band | Notes |
|-------|------|--------|
| Build (one-time) | **$3,500–$7,000** | Strategy sprint + CRM starter + outbound foundation |
| Run (monthly) | **$2,500–$4,500/mo** | Operate sequences + weekly held-meeting truth check |
| Optional hybrid | Base in band + **$150–$400** per held qualified meeting | Switches on after pilot; lowers base |

Undercuts PeopleLinx-style catering entry (~$2.5k+) on scope honesty for independents; sits in the Clay-agency / starter-outbound band Apero vacated below $7.5k/mo.

## Finish line

Owner can open the CRM and see who was contacted, who replied, who booked, who **held**, and what pipeline that created — on domains and lists they own.

## Transfer

Runbooks for list refresh, sequence edits, and the weekly booked-vs-held review. Engagement ends; motion stays.
