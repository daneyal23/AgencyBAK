# Demand System

## Tagline

Same accounts. Every channel. One CRM truth.

## For whom

Teams already sold on (or graduating from) GTM Machine who need inbound and paid to talk to the **same** TAM as outbound — one brand story, one measurement spine.

## What’s in

| Capability | Role in the package |
|------------|---------------------|
| Everything in GTM Machine | Strategy, engineering, outbound, RevOps, AI busywork |
| Websites **or** SEO **or** paid ads | Pick **one** demand surface in the pilot; expand after |
| Shared TAM | Lists, audiences, and CRM stages stay aligned across channels |

Default pilot choice: **website** (earn the next conversation) *or* **paid** (same accounts as outbound). SEO as the third path when search intent is the wedge.

## What’s out

Running all of web + SEO + paid at once in the pilot (reads as generalist; burns focus). PE-style embedded “do everything forever” retainers. Gulf WhatsApp-compliance builds (deferred with D2).

## Commercial (USD starting — draft)

| Layer | Band | Notes |
|-------|------|--------|
| Build (one-time) | **$8,000–$15,000** | Machine build + one demand surface (site *or* SEO foundation *or* ads setup) |
| Run (monthly) | **$5,500–$7,000/mo** | Ceiling stays **at or just under** Apero’s ~$7.5k Single-Channel floor |
| Media / tools | Pass-through | Ad spend, Clay credits, dialer seats itemised |

Ceiling discipline is intentional: Demand System is breadth under Main Street prices, not a soft climb into PE boutique retainers.

## Finish line

Prospects meet the same offer whether they reply to outbound, land on the site, or click an ad — and the CRM shows held meetings and pipeline by source without weekend Excel cleanup.

## Transfer

Channel runbooks plus the machine transfer from GTM Machine. Client keeps ad accounts, CMS/code, and analytics properties.
