Outbound that works is not a volume game. It is a short chain: the right accounts, a message that earns a reply, a send path that stays deliverable, and a CRM that tells you whether the meeting actually happened.

We build that chain in your accounts. You keep the domains, the inboxes, the lists, and the reporting. When the engagement ends, the pipeline does not leave with us.

## Under the hood (technical)

Sending: dedicated domains with SPF, DKIM, and DMARC; inbox warming before volume; suppression of customers and recent contacts; bounce and complaint thresholds that pause campaigns automatically. Cold email never uses the corporate primary domain.

Data: enrichment and validation before CRM load; duplicate checks; tiering so not every scraped row becomes a contact. Prefer client-owned Clay (or equivalent) workspaces and sequencers under your billing.

CRM: HubSpot, Zoho, GoHighLevel, or Pipedrive — whichever you already run. Custom properties for booked vs held; source and campaign attribution; dashboards your ops person can open without us.

Measurement lives in *your* CRM. We do not grade success on screenshots of a third-party calendar.

## How it works (finish → start)

1. **Finish** — Transfer: full ownership of domains, inboxes, lists, sequences, and CRM reports; short training so your team can operate and pause safely.
2. **Prove** — Live reporting of booked vs held; iterate copy and lists against what actually shows up.
3. **Scale** — Controlled volume once deliverability and show rates hold; segments stay separate so one bad list cannot burn the rest.
4. **Build** — Domains, warming, enrichment, sequences, CRM fields, and the first defensible account set.
5. **Start** — Operating truth: ICP, offer, economics, current tools, and what “a good meeting” means in your business.
